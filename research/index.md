<div class="research-card">

### [Consumer Engagement with AI-Powered Search Engines](papers/consumer-engagement-ai-search.md)
Given the trajectory toward a search environment in which consumers may rely on both traditional and AI-powered search engines to gather information, understanding the behavioral similarities and differences that emerge in consumers’ use of these two types of search platforms becomes imperative. This study examines how dual tracks of gratification-seeking can produce consequential differences in consumer behaviors, pushing research that employs uses and gratifications to consider how motivations may drive both platform choice and differential use simultaneously. 

**Venue:** Forthcoming in Journal of Advertising Research

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### [(Generative) AI and Measurement Validity](papers/ai-measurement-validity.md)
This study investigates the validity of computational attitude measures and provides researchers with best practices and considerations when applying them. We introduce the working of large language models and generative AI, investigate their precision in detecting attitude towards an ad and its strength, and contrast them with traditional survey measures to assess their convergent validity. 

**Venue:** International Communication Association (ICA) Conference paper

### [Multimodal LLMs in Human–AI Teams](projects/multimodal-llm-teams.md)
This project integrates core team‐process theory by mapping team- and taskwork behaviors to established LLM benchmarks. Building on Marks et al. (2001) framework, we specify measurable proxies that capture the ability of LLMs to act as a teammate. By benchmarking models along dimensions laid out by Marks et al. (2001), we create an architecture that can model an “ideal LLM teammate,” combining the best aspects of each model through LLM routing.  

**Status:** In progress

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### [Tracking Generative AI Discourse on Twitter/X](projects/twitter-genai-discourse.md)
The goal of this research is to investigate how GenAI / LLM discourse has changed on Twitter over the past three years (between 02/2022 and 02/2025), a primary domain for research on the sharing and consumption of AI-related information. Analyzing this period allows us to observe how the discourse has shifted from pre-ChatGPT release (11/30/2022) to post-release and beyond, to gain more granular insights

**Status:** Conference presentation, Revising for publication

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### [Meta-Analysis on The Effects of AI-Disclosure on Persuasive Outcomes](projects/disclosure-meta-analysis.md)
The current study examines how disclosures of GenAI use influence the persuasiveness of messaging by way of meta-analysis. Relying on theories of persuasive communication, this review summarizes findings from 26 papers (k = 34, N = 8,511) surrounding the effect of AI-disclosure labels on attitudes, intentions, and behaviors – conceptualized together as persuasion.

**Status:** Conference presentation, Revising for publication
